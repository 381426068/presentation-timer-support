PRIVACY POLICY · OCTOBER 23, 2026

Your time stays yours.

Presentation Timer is an offline Mac app. Its timer features do not require an account or an internet connection.

Information the app handles

The app stores your chosen interface language and window position on your Mac using Apple’s local settings storage. Timer operation and display happen on your device. The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or presentation content, and it does not send timer data to our servers.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data from the app with advertisers or data brokers. If you contact support by email, your email and the information you choose to send are used only to respond to your request.

Storage and control

Local settings remain on your Mac until you change or remove them. You may delete the app and its locally stored settings through macOS. Support correspondence can be deleted on request.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version and its date will appear on this page. For privacy questions, email 435952144@qq.com.
