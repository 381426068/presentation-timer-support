Keep your time in sight.

Presentation Timer helps you stay on pace during a talk, a class, or a rehearsal. Here’s how to get started and where to ask for help.

01

Start a timer

Choose Countdown or Target Time, set your duration or future date, then press Start. Count Up and Clock are also available.

02

Use the floating timer

Drag it to move, resize from an edge, and double-click to bring back settings. The app remembers its last visible position.

03

Choose a look

Switch between dark, light, transparent, frosted glass, and colorful styles. Colorful themes include both light and dark options.

04

Track overtime

Turn on “Keep tracking overtime after zero” before your talk. Pause when finished to retain the final overtime result.
